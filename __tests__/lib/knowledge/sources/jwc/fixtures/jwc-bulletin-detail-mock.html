Hull War, Piracy, Terrorism and Related Perils — Listed Areas

Published: 2025-01-15

The Joint War Committee has reviewed the current war risk situation. The following areas remain on the listed areas: Black Sea, Red Sea, and Gulf of Aden.

Vessels transiting these waters should maintain heightened vigilance. Additional war risk premium may apply for routes through the Persian Gulf and Indian Ocean.
